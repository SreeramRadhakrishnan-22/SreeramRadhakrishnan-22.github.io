About

I am a final-year undergraduate in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, where I conduct research at Brainseek Lab under the guidance of Prof. Gopalakrishnan Srinivasan.

My work sits at the intersection of computer architecture and neuromorphic computing. I am currently working on the architecture and RTL design of a dataflow-based, dual-sparse neuromorphic accelerator being developed at Brainseek Lab, IIT Madras.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing chess and basketball, and I am rarely without music, as the headphones in my photo will confirm. I also love travelling and trekking in the mountains.

A full account of my work is available in my curriculum vitae.
